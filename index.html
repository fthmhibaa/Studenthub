Effective Learning & Study Tips

Adopt modern techniques to maximize study efficiency and stay motivated.

Time Management

Use the Pomodoro Technique: Work for 25 minutes uninterrupted, followed by a 5-minute break. This retains high focus without burning out.

  • Prioritize tasks by urgency
  • Set clear daily study targets

Exam Preparation

Focus on Active Recall and solving previous year question papers under realistic timed constraints rather than passive reading.

  • Make concise revision flashcards
  • Simulate exam conditions

Effective Learning

Apply the Feynman Technique: Explain complex concepts in simple plain English as if teaching a beginner to pinpoint knowledge gaps.

  • Teach concepts to peers
  • Connect ideas visually with mind maps
